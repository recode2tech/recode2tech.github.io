Your documents and images are private. All PDF-to-Image conversions and Image-to-PDF creations happen entirely on your local device. We NEVER upload, transmit, view, or store your documents, photos, or files on any remote cloud or server.

We are committed to protecting your personal privacy. This policy outlines how information is handled when you download, install, and use our Application.

1. Information We Collect and Process

A. Document Files & Media (100% Local)

The Application allows you to import PDF files, extract or convert pages to JPG/PNG/WebP, and assemble pictures into new PDF documents.

  • No Server Uploads: Document processing takes place entirely within your device's local memory and sandbox storage.
  • No Access to File Contents: The Service Provider has no access to your document contents, file names, or generated images.
  • Local History & Cache: Conversion history and cached files reside exclusively on your local device and can be cleared at any time via the in-app settings.

B. No User Accounts Required

You do not need to register, create an account, or provide personal credentials (such as your name, email, or password) to use the core features of the Application.

C. Automatically Collected Technical & Diagnostic Data

To maintain app reliability, troubleshoot technical errors, and support free usage through advertising, the Application utilizes trusted third-party SDKs that may collect technical and diagnostic data:

Firebase Crashlytics (Google LLC)

We use Firebase Crashlytics to monitor application stability, detect crashes, and resolve bugs. Crashlytics collects technical crash logs, stack traces, device hardware models, operating system version, and device performance metrics at the moment of a crash.

Crashlytics does not collect your documents, images, or personally identifiable data.
Learn more: Firebase Crashlytics Terms • Firebase Privacy & Security

AdMob may collect and process information such as your device's Advertising ID (Google Advertising ID / IDFA), approximate location (IP-based), and ad interaction metrics to deliver relevant advertisements and combat ad fraud.

2. Device Permissions and Why We Need Them

The Application may request the following permissions on your mobile device:

  • Storage / Media Photos Access (READ_MEDIA_IMAGES / Scoped Storage): Required solely to allow you to select PDF files from your device and save exported images or converted PDFs to your device storage or photo gallery.
  • Internet Access (INTERNET / ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE): Required to load advertisements (AdMob), deliver crash reports to developers (Firebase Crashlytics), and authenticate in-app purchases (Google Play Billing). Core document conversion functions work completely offline without internet access.

3. Data Retention and User Control

Your Device Data: Converted files and history records remain on your device until you choose to delete them. You can delete individual conversion records, tap "Clear History" in Settings, or uninstall the Application to remove all local data.

Crash & Diagnostic Logs: Crash reports collected by Firebase Crashlytics are retained according to Google's standard retention schedules (typically up to 90 days) and are used strictly for identifying and resolving software bugs.

4. Advertising and Tracking Choices

Users who prefer not to see personalized advertisements can opt out at any time through their device settings:

Because all PDF and image processing occurs strictly on your device, your private documents never leave your possession. Any transmission of diagnostic or advertising data between your device and Google services is protected using standard Secure Sockets Layer / Transport Layer Security (TLS/HTTPS) encryption protocols.
